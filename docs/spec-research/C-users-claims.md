# Track C — Claims (the claims the proposals depend on most)

A reviewer will re-open every source. Each claim is one sentence, followed by the URL and the
exact supporting sentence from that page, on the same line so it can be mechanically checked.
Access date for all: 2026-10-28.

1. **NiftyFifty's own terms and about pages do not license its source code under any open licence.** — https://niftyfiftyquilting.com/terms — "Built with care by quilters, for quilters. © 2026 NiftyFifty. All rights reserved."

2. **The three recurring complaints about EQ8 are price, learning curve and desktop lock-in.** — https://niftyfiftyquilting.com/quilt-design-software/eq8-alternatives — "The three complaints that come up over and over in quilting forums: the $239.95 price, the learning curve (the most common word is 'frustrating'), and being chained to a desktop — EQ8 has no iPad, phone, or Chromebook version, and running it on both a Mac and a PC requires a paid migration kit."

3. **Electric Quilt 8 has no tablet, phone or Chromebook version.** — https://quiltler.com/eq8-alternative — "Electric Quilt 8 runs only on Windows and macOS desktops and laptops. There's no iPad app, no iPhone app, no Android or Chromebook version."

4. **Electric Quilt 8's project files are proprietary, so other tools cannot open them.** — https://quiltler.com/eq8-alternative — "Because EQ8 project files are proprietary, you simply recreate your design in Quiltler"

5. **A Fit-to-page print setting silently shrinks templates and ruins the finished quilt.** — https://mrsquilty.com/blogs/news/why-free-placemat-pdfs-print-wrong-and-the-60-second-fix — "A pattern printed at 96% does not look wrong at the first seam; it looks wrong at the last one, when the border no longer reaches the corner."

6. **Quarter Inch prints templates at true size with a calibration square.** — https://quarterinch.app/ — "Templates and foundations, with a 1″ test square on every page."

7. **Even in metric countries, quilters work in inches.** — https://kohatupatterns.com/blog/modern-quilting-guides/why-quilting-patterns-tools-and-tutorials-are-typically-designed-in-inches — "Even in countries that use the metric system, quilters often adapt to using inches for the sake of convenience and consistency."

8. **EQ8's centimetre option does not convert an existing design.** — https://support.electricquilt.com/articles/adjusting-for-inches-or-centimeters — "EQ8 does not calculate conversions if this option is changed after you have designed a quilt."

9. **Interactive targets must be at least 24 by 24 CSS pixels (WCAG 2.2 Level AA).** — https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html — "The size of the target for pointer inputs is at least 24 by 24 CSS pixels, except when:"

10. **Older web users need text resizable to 200 percent.** — https://www.w3.org/WAI/older-users/developing — "text can be resized without assistive technology up to 200 percent without loss of content or functionality"

11. **Colour-vision deficiency affects about 1 in 12 men and 1 in 200 women.** — https://www.colourblindawareness.org/colour-blindness — "Colour (color) blindness (colour vision deficiency, or CVD) affects approximately 1 in 12 men (8%) and 1 in 200 women."

12. **Usability testing should use no more than five users and iterate.** — https://www.nngroup.com/articles/why-you-only-need-to-test-with-5-users — "Elaborate usability tests are a waste of resources. The best results come from testing no more than 5 users and running as many small tests as you can afford."
