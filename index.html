librequilt

Free and open-source quilt design: block drawing, quilt layout, and yardage and cutting charts.

This is an early skeleton

There is no quilt editor yet. This page exists to prove that the project builds, runs and stays on your device. The work still to come is listed in the implementation plan in the repository.

What is working

Your designs stay with you

librequilt has no account, no server and no analytics. It does not send your designs, photographs or activity anywhere. Loading this page asks its host for the application files, and nothing else.
